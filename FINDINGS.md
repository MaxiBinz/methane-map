# Findings

The initial investigation executed on 20 real NASA EMIT V002 full granules in
one bounded Permian Basin region. They span 16 dates, 2023-12-24–2025-09-22.
The executed sample contains 15,917,415 usable **pixel-observations**, not that
many distinct geographic locations.

The sixteen search granules yielded four screened components; all four associate
with published observations. Across the whole sample there are six Category E
components and eight Category C components. All eight C components are in the
second known-positive validation scene. None is established as a novel methane
emission. These are component counts, not unique plume or source counts.

The conservative baseline missed the first positive scene and associated only
one of ten reference heads within 300 m in the second. Diagnostics traced misses
to cloud buffering, sensor/background thresholds and fragmented small supports.
Lower thresholds recover more known signals and many more components, including
in a catalog-negative control. These results cannot establish catalog completeness
or calibrated false-positive rates.

Every retained component has a dossier, catalog comparison, automated adversarial
assessment and map-panel visual review. Two pairs may be fragmented features;
two ambiguous components have known same-acquisition sources within 1 km. All
eight ambiguous components remain Category C. No facility, emission rate, annual
emission total or independent new-source corroboration is claimed.

The frozen numerical results reproduce exactly and 56 software tests pass.
Detailed evidence: `reports/validation_report.md`, `outputs/completion_audit.json`,
`outputs/adversarial_audit.json`, `outputs/known_diagnostics.json`.
